Microduck Guide Sans is a renamed, static, fixed-text subset of Noto Sans SC.
It is included to reproduce the Chinese print pack and placement guide without
depending on fonts installed on the user's machine. It is not a general Chinese
font. Use a full licensed font with generate_landmarks.py --font when adding
arbitrary new text that is not present in the original guide.
